# Phase 8: hero clip

One simulated VTOL delivery visit over real Pittsburgh terrain and buildings, rendered in
Gazebo and cut into a 60 to 90 s clip with a HUD built from the flight logs. Every vehicle, pad
and network number in it is a placeholder (spec A01); the clip says "simulated".

## 1. Render check

`px4/tools/render_check.sh <out> 60` starts a headless Gazebo server on a generated block city
(1500 boxes, shadows on), with three 1280x720 cameras at 30 Hz and the 320x240 10 Hz marker
camera, and streams the three large cameras into H.264 through `px4/tools/frame_recorder.py`.
Frames go from the camera topic straight into ffmpeg's stdin; nothing uncompressed is written.
ffmpeg is the static binary from the `imageio-ffmpeg` wheel in the px4 venv (none on the host).

| Renderer | Frames per wall second, each 720p camera | Real-time factor | Missing sim-time frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPU: Mesa 23.2.1 D3D12 driver on an RTX 4070 SUPER (WSL2 GPU passthrough, EGL headless) | 19.2 | 0.63 | 0 of 1146 |
| Software (`LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE=1`, llvmpipe) | 9.8 | 0.32 | 0 of 585 |

60 wall seconds each, ogre2. The sensors system holds the sim step until a due frame is
rendered, so the real-time factor drops instead of frames being skipped: frame stamps step by
32 or 36 ms (the 4 ms physics step), 30.3 Hz on average. The edit samples an exact 30 Hz
sim-time grid by taking the latest frame at or before each tick.

With the GPU a 250 s visit takes about 400 s of wall time with three cameras. Rendering is not a
blocker, and the GPU path is used.

## 3. Corridor world

`pipeline/run.sh hero world` builds the world offline from the pipeline cache: the OSM
Pennsylvania extract and the two USGS 3DEP 1 arc-second tiles, the same files in
`pipeline/data/raw/manifest.json`. No new data source is involved; building footprints come
from the same extract. The command writes into `px4/sim/hero/`, which is git-ignored and
regenerated by it. `pipeline/hero/out/world_manifest.json` is committed and records the input
sha256s, the counts below and the sha256 of every generated file. Placeholders are in
`pipeline/hero/hero.yaml`.

| Part | How it is built | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain | 30 m grid over 300 m either side of the lane and 500 m around each pad, bilinear DEM heights; four meshes tinted by elevation band (below 235, 265, 300 m and above) | 8544 cells, 17 088 triangles |
| Pads | ground flat at the pad's DEM elevation for 25 m, blending back over 40 m; a 12 m concrete apron (collision) carrying the IN and OUT slabs and markers | 2 |
| Water | OSM water polygons cut to the corridor, flat at the median DEM elevation inside each (3DEP is hydro-flattened) plus 0.3 m; terrain under them is pushed below | 4 polygons: the Allegheny by pad A (216.3 m), the river by pad B (216.6 m) and two smaller water bodies along the lane (227.9 and 245.9 m); 285 triangles |
| Roads | drivable OSM ways as 6 to 14 m dark strips 0.4 m above the terrain mesh, cut every 15 m; bridges span between their ends; tunnels are left out | 849 ways, 17 016 triangles |
| Buildings | footprints within 150 m of the lane or 300 m of a pad, extruded from 1 m below the lowest ground under them | 1635 buildings, 26 875 triangles |

Building heights come from OSM `height`, else `building:levels` × 3 m, else 8 m. Both fallback
values are placeholders.

| Height source | Buildings |
|---|---|
| `height` tag | 106 |
| `building:levels` tag | 82 |
| Default 8 m | 1447 |

One more footprint touched a pad's 15 m clear circle and was left out: the warehouse around the
hub's pad point, since the pad is modelled as a ground slab.

The world has 61 264 triangles against a configured cap of 600 000. Footprints would be
simplified (0.5, 1, 2, then 4 m tolerance) if it went over; none were.

The terrain, water, roads and buildings are visual only. Only the aprons and spot slabs collide,
so physics costs what it did on the flat world.

Heights are world z from pad A's ground, which sits at 222.26 m NAVD88 (the world's
`spherical_coordinates` elevation). Pad B, Josh Steel, is at 224.74 m, so 2.48 m higher. The
lane's highest terrain is 350.89 m (`lanes.csr.bin` `max_terrain_m`, 18% of the way along), so
the constant cruise altitude is 350.89 + 60.96 − 222.26 = 189.59 m above pad A.

Running the command twice gives an identical manifest: every generated file is byte-identical.

## 4. Flight over terrain

`px4/run_flight.sh --airframe vtol --payload-kg 5 --seed 1 --world-dir px4/sim/hero` flies the
full Phase 7 VTOL visit over the generated world at 1x:

1. take off in multicopter mode from OUT at A, climb, transition;
2. fixed-wing cruise, back-transition;
3. land on IN at B by marker, release the pod, verify, hop, land on OUT, latch, verify, park.

It uses the Phase 7 airframe and fit-lane parameters unchanged, a 5 kg pod and the normal path
only. `run.json` records the px4/ commit (`script_git`), `run_flight.sh`'s last commit, the
world generator's commit (`world_git`) and the hash of the world flown.

What the terrain changed (spec A95):

- **Constant cruise altitude** of 189.59 m above pad A: the lane's highest terrain plus the
  60.96 m airspace cruise height.
- **Pad ground heights.** Each pad's ground height is in `scene.json`. The approach height
  (6 m) is measured from the pad's own ground, which is 2.48 m higher at B. The pad
  controller's on-spot and pod-rest checks, and the spawn poses, use the same ground. On the
  flat world every pad's ground is 0, so nothing changes there.
- **Hop height** (2.5 m) is measured from where the drone stands on IN, not from the EKF
  origin. In run `clip_3` the baro height had drifted 0.9 m by the hop, and the drone hovered
  over OUT 1.2 to 1.8 m above the ground instead of 2.5 m. The OUT marker, 1 m beside the
  spot, stayed out of the camera's view, and the visit failed ("marker 4 not detected").
- **PX4's landing speed near B.** Without a rangefinder, PX4 slows its descent by height
  above home (pad A), not above B. Over the 2.48 m difference that changes the speed only in
  the last metres; there is no fix for it.
- **Barometer datum (fixed in this repo, as in A67).** The world is at pad A's real
  elevation, 222.26 m NAVD88, and Gazebo's GPS reports that.
  - *Failure:* Gazebo's air pressure sensor reads pressure at world z alone and ignores
    `<reference_altitude>`. PX4's baro-to-GNSS auto-calibration moved the baro by 222 m after
    the EKF origin was set. The EKF height jumped by 222 m at arming, the cruise setpoint lay
    below it, and PX4 disarmed without flying (runs `terrain_try1` and `terrain_try2`).
  - *Fix:* a per-run `SENS_BARO_QNH` (1040.37 hPa) makes PX4 read Gazebo's sea-level
    pressure as 222.26 m. Baro, GPS, EKF origin and home then agree from boot. The baro's
    altitude scale differs from Gazebo's by about 0.5% (lapse × elevation / T0), roughly 1 m
    at cruise.

Run `terrain_try3` (`px4/tools/hero_check.py`), validator PASS:

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Outcome | COMPLETE, 926 s wall at 1x |
| Takeoff (climb to 189.6 m) / cruise / approach and landing at IN / hop (s) | 70.9 / 742.4 / 110.3 / 23.6 |
| EKF height minus truth (from the start), max over the flight | 1.38 m (mean −0.82 m over the lane; 0.16 m back on the ground at B) |
| Minimum clearance to terrain, lane | 63.4 m, at the ridge 1.7 km along (margin 30 m) |
| Minimum clearance to buildings, lane | 57.0 m, OSM way 535864533 on that ridge (margin 30 m) |
| Landing error IN / OUT | 11.8 / 7.1 cm (latch capture radius 15 cm) |

Height drift is the EKF's altitude above its origin minus the ground-truth climb, sampled at
20 Hz. Clearance to terrain is vertical, against the terrain mesh under the path. Clearance to
buildings is the 3D distance to the nearest extruded prism, sampled every 0.5 s. Both exclude
the pads' 15 m clear circles, where the drone stands on its pad. The minima over the whole
airborne flight equal the lane's. Neither margin is violated.

The four terrain flights flown at the final revision repeat these numbers:

| Measure | Range |
|---|---|
| Max height drift | 1.43 to 1.65 m |
| Minimum clearance to terrain | 62.7 to 64.0 m |
| Minimum clearance to buildings | 56.5 to 57.7 m |
| Landing error (all 10 landings) | within 2.7 to 14.0 cm |

## 5. Cameras

`run_flight.sh --cameras` adds three 1280×720, 30 Hz render cameras (`px4/tools/hero_cameras.py`,
placement in `hero.yaml` `cameras`). Each camera is its own static model, with no inertia and no
collision:

- **Chase:** posed every sim step by `dd_chase_camera` (`px4/sim/systems`, a Gazebo system
  built by `px4/tools/build_gz_systems.sh`). It sits 9 m behind the drone along its ground
  track, 3.5 m up and 2.5 m to the side, following with a 0.3 s lag in sim time.
- **Pad close-up:** fixed 20 m from pad B, looking at the IN and OUT spots.
- **High-wide:** fixed 600 m up behind pad A, looking down the lane.

The downward marker camera on the drone is unchanged. `px4/tools/frame_recorder.py` subscribes
to the three topics and writes each frame's raw RGB straight into its own ffmpeg (H.264,
CRF 16), with the frame's sim-time stamp in a CSV. The video is therefore timed by sim time,
whatever the wall speed.

| Measure | Run `clip_5` | Run `clip_6` |
|---|---|---|
| Frames per wall second, each camera | 23.4 | 22.9 |
| Real-time factor | 0.77 | 0.76 |
| Missing sim-time frames | 0 of 31 598 | 0 of 29 698 |
| Wall time (1x) | 1285 s | 1237 s |
| Peak disk of the camera streams (three H.264 masters) | 219 MB | 215 MB |
| Peak RSS, whole run | 3565 MB | 3463 MB |

**With and without cameras** (seed 1, the same revision `a420196`):

| Run | Cameras | Visit, mission to parked (s) | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|

`clip_5`'s extra minute is one hover at the holding fix, not a camera effect. The drone was at
the fix 0.3 m from its centre at 826 s, but PX4's estimate showed a steady −0.4 m/s vertical
velocity, 0.4 m off the setpoint height, for 65 s. The agent's arrival check (within 0.3 m
vertically, under 0.5 m/s) only passed at 891 s. The cameras are massless, kinematic models,
and `clip_6`, which also had them, matched the no-camera flight within 0.5%. It is the same
height estimator drift as the hop failure above (A96). It is left as found: loosening the
arrival check is not this phase's call.

## 6. Edit and HUD

`px4/tools/hero_edit.py` cuts the clip from one camera run by the rules in `hero.yaml` `edit`.
Segments start at anchors in the event log: a message, a drone transition, a VTOL phase, or
the drone reaching 8.5 m above pad B. Each names a camera and plays at 1x, or is compressed to
a set length with "x N" on the HUD. Output frames sample an exact 30 Hz grid of sim time,
scaled by N, in whole microseconds. Each takes the camera frame with the latest stamp at or
before its time.

What plays at 1x, and what is compressed (spec A97):

- **At 1x:** the mission and departure handshake and liftoff, the transition to fixed wing, the
  back-transition, the landing request and grant, the last 8 s of the landing on IN, release
  and verify, the end of the hop and touchdown on OUT, and the latch report to parking.
- **Compressed, with "x N":** the climb (x23), the cruise (x90, half on the overview camera with
  a ring drawn at the drone's logged position), the flight to the fix, the descent from
  190 m (x23), the wait for the marker lock, most of the hop and the latch wait.

The brief asked for everything but the cruise at 1x. The visit spends 190 s outside the
cruise, mostly climbing to and descending from 190 m, so that cannot fit 60 to 90 s.

The HUD is drawn from the logs only:

- **States:** drone state and VTOL phase, and pad A's and pad B's visit states.
- **Messages:** the last three handshake messages with their send times.
- **Sim timer:** from MISSION_ASSIGN.
- **Height above the terrain mesh.**
- **Battery:** PX4's battery %. PX4's SITL battery drains with time, not load (A57), and floors
  at 50%; the HUD says so.
- **Drone vs truck panel:** "simulation, placeholder inputs", from the demo scenario's own
  `deliveries.csv` at seed 42 (`pipeline/run.sh hero panel`, `pipeline/hero/out/panel.json`).
  Lane 5 has 21 drone-delivered orders. Their median door to door is 18.2 min; the median
  order splits into 1.0 min of pod wait, 2.2 min of queue, 2.6 min of pad service and 12.4 min
  of flight. The truck takes a median 41.3 min on the same orders.
- **Cards:** a 3 s title card (lane, 11.1 km, airframe, "simulated") and a 4 s closing card
  (this visit's 16 min, the panel's medians, the same label).

`px4/tests/test_hero_hud.py <run>` checks the HUD of every frame (`<run>/edit/hud_timeline.json`):

- **State changes:** every HUD state change lies within one frame after the log event that
  causes it. Every logged state is on screen by the first frame after it, unless another event
  in the same tick replaced it.
- **Messages:** every message shows its log send time exactly, appears first on the first frame
  at or after it, and keeps send order.
- **Battery:** the battery figure is the sample's.
- **Panel:** the panel equals a fresh `sim run` of the demo at seed 42.

Results:

- **Final clip (run `clip_6`):** 2260 HUD frames, 24 state events, 19 messages shown, PASS.
  Run `clip_5` also passes.
- **Earlier failures:** the test first failed twice on transitions sharing one sim tick (a test
  error) and on two frames whose battery came from the neighbouring sample. Frame times are now
  whole microseconds everywhere.
- **Mutations:** a state change shifted one frame early and a panel figure off by 1 s were both
  caught.

The clip is `video/hero_clip.mp4` (git-ignored): 82.3 s, 1280×720, 30 fps, H.264, no audio,
6.0 MB. It is cut from run `clip_6`. Stills from it: `docs/media/hero_transition.png`,
`docs/media/hero_overview.png`, `docs/media/hero_landing.png`. `docs/media/hero_track.png` maps
the run's ground-truth track over the corridor world, with the height along the lane against the
terrain and buildings (`pipeline/run.sh hero map "$PWD/px4/runs/hero/clip_6"`).

## 7. Terrain against flat

The same lane, VTOL, 5 kg, 1x, two repeats each, all at revision `a420196`
(`px4/tools/run_batch.sh hero_check …`, `pipeline/run.sh hero terrain-check`,
`pipeline/hero/out/terrain_check.md`):

- **Flat:** the Phase 7 world, 60.96 m cruise.
- **Terrain:** the corridor world, 189.59 m cruise above pad A.

The model is the core's `sim flight-model` with the fitted VTOL profile on the SITL vehicle,
compared phase by phase as in Phase 7's held-out validation. Model error is predicted over
measured, minus 1.

| Run | Visit (s) | Takeoff (s) | Lane (s) | Approach + landing (s) | Hop (s) | Energy proxy (kJ) | Model time error | Model energy error | Landing IN / OUT (cm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| heroflat_1 | 848.2 | 25.9 | 748.5 | 50.0 | 23.9 | 574.7 | −0.2% | +0.3% | 13.3 / 2.8 |
| heroflat_2 | 856.2 | 27.4 | 754.1 | 47.8 | 26.9 | 575.0 | −1.1% | +0.2% | 6.0 / 2.8 |
| heroterrain_1 | 956.7 | 70.5 | 742.3 | 119.7 | 24.3 | 772.9 | −11.5% | −25.4% | 7.6 / 13.4 |
| heroterrain_2 | 952.6 | 68.6 | 742.1 | 120.6 | 21.4 | 767.7 | −11.1% | −24.9% | 11.5 / 9.0 |
| sim flight model | 846.8 | 26.2 | 746.6 | 49.9 | 24.2 | 576.5 | | | |

Over flat ground the model holds on an 11.1 km lane it was not fitted on: 0.6% in time and 0.3%
in energy proxy, mean of two.

Terrain costs 102 s (+12%) and 195 kJ (+34%) more than flat:

- **Takeoff:** +43 s and +80 kJ, for the climb to 190 m instead of 61 m.
- **Approach:** +71 s and +120 kJ, for the descent from 190 m.
- **Lane and hop:** about the same.

What the model leaves out:

- **Altitude:** the core has none (A03). Its takeoff and approach are fixed times at hover
  power, fitted on 61 m, so it cannot charge the climb to and descent from a cruise altitude set
  by terrain. It underestimates this visit by 11% in time and 25% in energy.
- **Pad elevations:** with pads at different elevations, the climb and descent no longer
  cancel; here they differ by only 2.5 m.
- **Height of the cruise itself:** air density falls about 1.5% from 61 m to 190 m above a 222 m
  pad, which the proxy (fixed ρ = 1.225 kg/m³) also ignores.

## Limitations

- **One run per clip.** The clip is one visit. Gazebo and PX4 are not seeded, so a rerun
  differs in timing (`clip_5` against `clip_6`).
- **Estimator drift.** The barometric height wanders by up to 1.65 m over the 190 m climb and
  descent. It cost one hop (fixed) and one minute-long hover at the fix (open).
- **Placeholders.** Building heights are placeholders for 1447 of the 1635 buildings. Roads
  are flat strips, bridges straight ramps.
- **Collision.** Nothing but the pads collides, so the clearance check is the only guard
  against terrain and buildings.
- **No wind** and no failure branches.
- **Battery.** The HUD battery is PX4's time-based SITL battery, not the energy proxy.
